# International Conference on Political Participation and Society (IC2PS-26)

## 📅 12–13 September 2026

### 📍 Muscat, Oman

### 🌐 Hybrid Conference

---

## About IC2PS-26

The **International Conference on Political Participation and Society (IC2PS-26)** is an international academic platform organized by the **Asian Society for Publication, Higher Education & Research (ASPHER)**.

The conference brings together researchers, academicians, educators, professionals, and practitioners from around the world to exchange knowledge, present original research, explore emerging developments, and engage in meaningful academic and professional discussions.

IC2PS-26 provides an opportunity to promote interdisciplinary collaboration, strengthen international research networks, and contribute to the advancement of knowledge in the fields of political participation and society.

---

## 🎯 Conference Objectives

IC2PS-26 aims to:

* Advance global scholarship and emerging research.
* Encourage interdisciplinary dialogue and collaboration.
* Provide opportunities for research presentations and knowledge exchange.
* Strengthen international academic and professional networks.
* Promote innovative ideas and impactful research.
* Foster collaboration between academia, industry, and professional organizations.
* Support the dissemination and visibility of high-quality research.

---

## 📚 Conference Topics

The conference focuses on important areas related to politics, society, governance, and public participation, including:

### Track 01 — The Dynamics of Political Participation

Exploring the processes, structures, and evolving forms of political participation in contemporary societies.

### Track 02 — Civic Engagement in Contemporary Society

Examining civic involvement, public engagement, and the role of citizens in shaping society.

### Track 03 — Representation and Inclusivity in Politics

Addressing political representation, inclusion, diversity, and participation across different communities.

### Track 04 — Governance and Public Policy: A Critical Analysis

Exploring governance systems, public policy, institutional structures, and contemporary policy challenges.

### Track 05 — Rights and Justice in Political Contexts

Examining human rights, social justice, equality, and political frameworks influencing rights and justice.

* Reviewer Selection
* Double-Blind Review
